# `mboostrs` Status Against the Knowledge Discovery Scorecard

This page consolidates the measured status of `mboostrs` under `mboostrs-scorecard/2.1` ([scorecard.md](scorecard.md)). Numerical grades in the table come from the six-library automated held-out comparison ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb), [record](../book/appendix/records/heldout_fit.md)), which scored six of the twelve dimensions under one pre-registered protocol. Dimensions not exercised by that protocol remain `unmeasured` on the canonical card, with supporting evidence from separate task-specific studies summarized alongside them. No cross-study composite score is computed.

## Consolidated Profile

|---|---|---|---|
| **D1a Relationship recovery (prescribed specification)** | **Unmeasured** *(task-specific proxies)* | Hostile-workflow agents identified every planted trap (18/18 runs; [hostile workflows](../book/benchmarks/hostile_workflows_v4.ipynb)); the updated job-training analyst selected a valid adjustment set on 8/8 replicates ([agent records](../book/appendix/records/agent_benchmarks.md)). | No study yet isolates recovery strictly of *open* terms left unspecified by a prescribed rough formula under Track A. |

## Priority Improvements

1. **Confirm on a fully independent held-back task suite (D11):** Because the real-data comparison reused the 95 tables that informed `mb.fit` improvements (`DEVIATIONS.md` #5), an independent held-back benchmark suite is needed to verify real-data accuracy without development exposure.
2. **Effect-interval calibration and temporal shift coverage (D4, D9):** Propagate first-stage nuisance uncertainty into heterogeneous treatment-effect intervals (`effect_model`), support native `time=` rolling validation in `mb.fit`, and benchmark `ConformalReliabilityWarning` under temporal shift.
3. **Large-$n$ stress benchmark and sub-second 50k sessions (D5, D6):** Measure 5M-row scalability under a frozen protocol and reduce 50k × 20 automated follow-ups below 1.0 s.
4. **Close unmeasured scorecard dimensions (D1a, D3, D7, D8):** Run a dedicated Track A open-structure benchmark, multi-analyst agent comparisons with the new fast search, and a structured human usability study.

## Evidence Scope

- Numerical scores in the table reflect the automated default-settings held-out comparison; the 95 real datasets in that study were also used during `mb.fit` development, which favors `mboostrs` relative to unseen task families.
- Tuned comparative studies ([competitive shootout](../book/benchmarks/competitive_shootout.ipynb), [capacity chooser](../book/benchmarks/chooser_shootout.ipynb), [interaction capacity](../book/benchmarks/interaction_capacity.ipynb)) and specialized count/distributional studies ([count models](../book/appendix/records/count_models.md), [spread and distributional](../book/appendix/records/spread_and_distributional.md)) keep their own protocols and are not pooled into version 2.1 grades.
- Code improvements do not alter any recorded grade until measured by a fresh evaluation under a frozen protocol.
