# `mboostrs` Status Against the Knowledge Discovery Scorecard

This page consolidates the measured status of `mboostrs` under `mboostrs-scorecard/2.1` ([scorecard.md](scorecard.md)). Numerical grades in the table come from the six-library automated held-out comparison ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb), [record](../book/appendix/records/heldout_fit.md)), which scored six of the twelve dimensions under one pre-registered protocol. Dimensions not exercised by that protocol remain `unmeasured` on the canonical card, with supporting evidence from separate task-specific studies summarized alongside them. No cross-study composite score is computed.

## Consolidated Profile

| Dimension | Status (`mboostrs-scorecard/2.1`) | Primary Evidence | Principal Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1a Relationship recovery (prescribed specification)** | **Unmeasured** *(task-specific proxies)* | Hostile-workflow agents identified every planted trap (18/18 runs; [hostile workflows](../book/benchmarks/hostile_workflows_v4.ipynb)); the updated job-training analyst selected a valid adjustment set on 8/8 replicates ([agent records](../book/appendix/records/agent_benchmarks.md)). | No study yet isolates recovery strictly of *open* terms left unspecified by a prescribed rough formula under Track A. |
| **D1b Relationship discovery (data alone)** | **Scored 3 / 5** *(held-out comparison)* | On planted tasks with no formula supplied, a common held-out permutation test recovered 70/80 true predictors (0/10 interactions) at 1.4% FDR with 5/5 silent null tasks; tree libraries recovered 80/80 at 31–46% FDR ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb)). | Default `mb.fit` does not adopt interaction terms, and its reported formula retains unpruned noise terms (64.5% FDR under native formula scoring). |
| **D2 Causal humility and support** | **Unmeasured** *(mixed workflow evidence)* | Hostile-workflow runs framed confounding as observational, explained the marginal-to-conditional sign reversal (−1.35 to +1.6), and flagged target leakage ([hostile workflows](../book/benchmarks/hostile_workflows_v4.ipynb)). Job-training runs achieved 8/8 valid adjustment sets with zero false no-effect claims ([causal discovery](../book/benchmarks/causal_discovery.ipynb)). | Small-sample thin-overlap ATE estimation remains unstable ($n = 1{,}500$), and causal identification across broader observational designs is unmeasured. |
| **D3 Explanation quality** | **Unmeasured** *(task-specific evidence)* | Hostile-workflow reports accurately characterized missingness, leakage, heteroscedasticity, and confounding with explicit limitations ([hostile workflows](../book/benchmarks/hostile_workflows_v4.ipynb)). | General report faithfulness and completeness across broader domains and human evaluators remain untested. |
| **D4 Importance quantification** | **Unmeasured** *(needs attention)* | In matched-nuisance simulations, `mb.aipw` achieved \$434 median absolute ATE error with 9/10 nominal 90% coverage ([causal estimators](../book/benchmarks/causal_estimator_benchmark.ipynb)). In the job-training battery, nominal 90% per-applicant effect intervals covered 61–66% and ATE intervals covered 75% ([agent records](../book/appendix/records/agent_benchmarks.md)). | Second-stage heterogeneous-effect intervals omit first-stage nuisance uncertainty, causing undercoverage for per-unit treatment effects. |
| **D5 Iteration speed** | **Scored 2 / 5** *(held-out comparison)* | On the standardized 50,000 × 20 analyst session (10 follow-up fits with prediction and permutation importance), median latency was 34.4 s versus 0.58 s (XGBoost), 0.76 s (LightGBM), 6.7 s (CatBoost), and 67.5 s (EBM) ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb)). Single additive fits run in 0.41 s at 90,000 rows ([scaling](../book/benchmarks/public_scaling.ipynb)). | Automatic search and follow-up refits remain tens of seconds at 50k rows, and agent sessions with the bundled guide consumed more fits (~686 vs. ~378 without it) for the same verdicts. |
| **D6 Robustness across conditions** | **Scored 2 / 5** *(held-out comparison)* | Completed 202 of 285 real-data runs (71%) in the held-out comparison, versus 260/285 for EBM and 285/285 for CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, and the ridge/logistic baseline. Achieved the lowest loss of all six libraries on the small-sample ($n = 200$) planted task ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb)). | Default `mb.fit` fails on missing values, tables where every row contains a missing entry, and unseen categorical levels at prediction time. |
| **D7 Human-friendliness** | **Unmeasured** | Executable tutorials, diagnostic warnings, and case studies document standard workflows, but time-to-first-discovery and error recovery have not been measured with human analysts. | Requires a structured human task study; time-ordered rolling validation and quantile crossing still require manual setup in some workflows. |
| **D8 Agent-friendliness** | **Unmeasured** *(mixed workflow evidence)* | Autonomous agents completed the hostile-workflow, claim-cost, and causal batteries end to end ([agent records](../book/appendix/records/agent_benchmarks.md)). However, the guided arm took longer (~127 min vs. ~87 min unguided) with no accuracy gain. | Variation across multiple independent analysts per arm is unmeasured, and agent workflows need lower fit counts and faster convergence. |
| **D9 Uncertainty honesty (calibration)** | **Scored 4 / 5** *(held-out comparison)* | Split-conformal nominal 90% prediction intervals achieved 0.900 median coverage (96% of runs within 0.85–0.95) and remained uniform across heteroscedastic terciles (0.89 / 0.91 / 0.91), versus 0.73–0.79 for default tree quantile intervals ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb)). | Under temporal distribution shift (Beijing PM2.5 future year), nominal 80% bands covered 0.74 overall and 0.68 in winter across all libraries ([PM2.5 forecast](../book/benchmarks/pm25_forecast_competition.ipynb)). |
| **D10 Reproducibility and auditability** | **Scored 3 / 5** *(held-out comparison)* | Exact prediction reproduction on 10/10 repeated tasks (matched by all comparators), plus a structured `DecisionTrail` recording candidates, decisions, budgets, and seeds ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb)). | Independent one-command reproduction of a complete end-user discovery report (D10 level 4) has not yet been demonstrated. |
| **D11 Predictive validity** | **Scored 3 / 5** *(held-out comparison)* | Beat the prespecified ridge/logistic reference on 27/33 completed regression and 23/30 completed binary tasks (Wilcoxon $p = 3 \times 10^{-4}$ each), and achieved lowest loss on planted additive tasks ([held-out comparison](../book/benchmarks/heldout_competition.ipynb)). | Trailed CatBoost on 24/33 completed regression tasks (median gap to best complete library 35.3% regression, 12.6% binary) and missed pure interactions; tuned hybrid/capacity-selected fits narrow much of this gap ([shootout record](../book/appendix/records/competitive_shootout.md)). |

## Priority Improvements

1. **Real-data completion (D6) — implemented, pending full v2.1 rerun:** All 83 real-data failures traced to three causes: unseen levels across `predict`, lowered factor `bols`, and `conformal_interval` (45 runs); `na_action="omit"` dropping rows or passing test `NaN`/`None` into predictors and `sqrt`/`log` transforms (38 runs); and Polars `Object` dtype handling. The remediation defaults `mb.fit` to `na_action="impute"` (retaining observed-response rows during `autospec` search and adding training-mode categorical imputation) and `unseen_levels="average"`, cutting held-out `mb.fit` failures from **87 to 2** across 285 runs with a **0.806** paired geometric-mean test loss ratio (95% CI 0.647–0.977; 34 better, 22 tied, 13 worse). See [held-out remediation record](../benchmarks/2026-11-25-heldout-eyesores-remediation.md).
2. **Interaction capacity and formula fidelity (D1b, D11) — implemented, pending full v2.1 rerun:** The remediation defaults `mb.fit` to `interactions="adopt"`, `residual_tree="auto"` (reusing `MstopEvidence.fold_scores` to cut CV cost from `4 * n_folds` to `n_folds`, with held-out residual-tree detection and sparse active-column scoping), a 64-fit standard budget, and active-term pruning for `Fit.trail.final["formula"]` (>= 1% risk reduction or LSS partial-effect variance; `y ~ 1` on flat CV curves). On the 35 planted draws (S1–S7), permutation discovery reaches 80/80 predictors (10/10 interactions) at 1.2% FDR with 5/5 clean null tasks, reported-formula FDR drops from 64.5% to 2.4% (80/80 TPR, 10/10 interactions, 5/5 clean null tasks), and S2 test MSE drops from 9.32 to 0.375. On the worst-12 real regression tables vs. CatBoost, `energy_efficiency` improves from 12.19× to 1.90×, `airfoil_self_noise` from 10.93× to 2.78×, `grid_stability` from 4.85× to 2.92×, `california_housing` from 1.81× to 1.11×, and `pumadyn32nh` from 2.29× to 0.98×.
3. **Follow-up iteration speed (D5) — implemented, pending full v2.1 rerun:** Added `autospec(..., search="fast")` (single-pass held-out screening radar reaching **1.43 s median** and **#2 mean R² = 0.6387** across all 24 OpenML-CTR23 datasets with 600/600 completions in [benchmark_realdata](../book/cases/benchmark_realdata.ipynb)), the Rust `btree` histogram-subtraction engine (`SplitMethod::Histogram`, ~3× faster depth-5/6 trees), 10,000-row exhaustive pre-exploration capping, noise-column shape-probe skipping on wide tables, and deterministic 3,000-row search subsampling in `mb.fit` (lowering the 50,000 × 20 ten-step session median from 34.4 s to 5.1 s at 32 fits and 9.4 s at 64 fits).
4. **Effect-interval calibration and temporal coverage (D4, D9):** Propagate first-stage nuisance uncertainty into heterogeneous treatment-effect intervals (`effect_model`) and support time-ordered validation (`time=`) and shift-aware conformal bands.
5. **Close unmeasured scorecard dimensions (D1a, D3, D7, D8):** Run a dedicated Track A open-structure benchmark, multi-analyst agent comparisons, and a structured human usability study.

## Evidence Scope

- Numerical scores in the table reflect the automated default-settings held-out comparison; the 95 real datasets in that study were also used during `mb.fit` budget development, which favors `mboostrs` relative to unseen task families.
- Tuned comparative studies ([competitive shootout](../book/benchmarks/competitive_shootout.ipynb), [capacity chooser](../book/benchmarks/chooser_shootout.ipynb), [interaction capacity](../book/benchmarks/interaction_capacity.ipynb)) and specialized count/distributional studies ([count models](../book/appendix/records/count_models.md), [spread and distributional](../book/appendix/records/spread_and_distributional.md)) keep their own protocols and are not pooled into version 2.1 grades.
- Code improvements do not alter any recorded grade until measured by a fresh evaluation under a frozen protocol.
