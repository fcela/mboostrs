# Production candidate decision

This decision precedes fresh confirmation. Keep the original primary candidate:
shape simplification ONLY, with the existing Gaussian.1/other-family.02 rates.
The rate-only and combined variants remain fully reported diagnostic controls.
Although the higher NBinomial rate improves the additive nonlinear development
control, it does not materially improve the original zero-inflated count-size
contrast and increases delivered mean error on the family-supplied genuine
interaction diagnostic. No learning-rate default change is selected.

The production candidate compares the generated count mean draft with its
linear backbone on the same five fitting folds at200 updates, preferring the
simpler draft within two paired fold standard errors. Numeric choice and shared
existing learning-rate lookup live in Rust and are bound to Python/R. Formula
planning remains in the frontend. Preserve mandatory variables, autogenerated
log transformations,candidate restrictions and budget admission. A new
simplify_mean=False option explicitly retains the prior draft. Gaussian,
Binomial and other non-count shape choices remain unchanged.

The unconditional genuine-interaction90% aspiration remains unestablished on
the ordinary control: every development model selects Gaussian and the changed
count branch does not execute. Preserve those8/10 outcomes and require
non-regression of the unchanged ordinary pipeline. The separately labelled
NBinomial-family diagnostic exercises the applicable branch and retains9/10
interactions for the old and primary procedures; use its previously scoped90%
and mean-MSE noninferiority checks on fresh draws. It is not data-only evidence.

All original1% width/cardinality gates and nonlinear/null/noise checks remain
as specified. The additive nonlinear count limitation in#679 is retained,
not declared fixed by this shape-only candidate. The fresh6608100–6608149 cohort
is generated only after validated production source,runner and complete
analysis are committed. No old development or confirmation record is renamed,
rescored into a preferred method,or overwritten. A failed fresh gate leaves
the old default pending further work.
