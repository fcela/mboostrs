# Benchmark records

This appendix collects concise technical records for the empirical studies summarized in Part V: study designs, split and budget specifications, key quantitative tables, and failure accounting. Immutable pre-registrations, SHA-256 protocol digests, raw JSONL logs, and verbatim agent transcripts remain archived in the repository under `docs/benchmarks/` and `docs/book/benchmarks/` for independent audit rather than being compiled verbatim into the book.

| Record | Scope | Companion chapters in Part V |

| [Competitive shootout, capacity selection, and predictive refresh](records/competitive_shootout.md) | 8-family shootout across 13 predictive and 4 causal panels, nested capacity selection, and fixed-tier refresh | [](../benchmarks/competitive_shootout.ipynb), [](../benchmarks/chooser_shootout.ipynb), [](../benchmarks/competitive_refresh.ipynb) |
